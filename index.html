How it works

Three steps.

You bring the judgement. The model copies it across the rest of your rows.

01

Upload labeled examples

A small CSV of rows you have already judged, with the column you care about filled in. A few dozen is often enough.

02

Upload rows to score

The same columns, minus the answer. This is the set you want scored, from a few hundred rows to a few thousand.

03

Get a predicted column back

Preview the scored table in the browser and download it as CSV, with a confidence score on every row.

Why it is different

No training run. No tuning.

Under the hood is a tabular foundation model. It was pre-trained on millions of synthetic tables, so it already knows how columns tend to relate to each other. Your examples go in as context, not as a training set.

That means a prediction takes seconds, not a sprint, and you can change your criteria and re-run without starting over.

Your criteria, not a fixed schema

Lead fit, risk flags, churn, priority, a quality grade. If you can label 50 rows, you can predict the rest.

Works on plain CSVs

Numbers, text categories, missing values. Columns are matched between the two files automatically.

Runs on your machine

The prediction app runs locally on a laptop CPU. Your data does not have to leave your network.
